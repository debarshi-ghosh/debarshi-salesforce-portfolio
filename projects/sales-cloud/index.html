Enterprise Sales Cloud Implementation

Connecting customer records, opportunity execution, territory planning and forecasting in a more consistent manufacturing sales operating model.

Business challenge

The confirmed project scope points to a need for a more connected and consistent way to manage manufacturing sales operations. Account, contact and opportunity activity needed to sit within a shared CRM workflow, with a clearer progression from opportunity management through sales execution and forecasting.

The inclusion of sales process automation, territory and forecast management, reporting, data migration and external integrations also points to a broader need: bring relevant customer and sales information together, reduce reliance on disconnected or manual steps, and give sales stakeholders a more dependable view of pipeline activity and performance.

This business context is inferred from the implementation scope confirmed for this portfolio. The resume does not state the client's exact pre-implementation process, source-system names or quantified baseline, so no such details are presented as facts.

Solution overview

Implemented Salesforce Sales Cloud capabilities to support the sales lifecycle in a manufacturing environment. The solution scope brought customer and opportunity management, process automation, custom user experience, connected systems, reporting and territory/forecast capabilities into one CRM delivery.

How the solution addressed the need

  • Connected customer and pipeline records: Account, contact and opportunity management provided a common place to maintain customer relationships and sales activity.
  • More consistent sales execution: Opportunity progression and sales process automation supported repeatable sales workflows.
  • Improved management visibility: Reports, dashboards, territory management and forecasting supported pipeline review and sales planning.
  • Fit-for-purpose user experience: Custom Lightning Web Components supported user journeys beyond standard CRM screens.
  • Connected and transitioned data: External integrations and data migration brought the required data and surrounding systems into the delivery scope.

Confirmed implementation scope

  • Opportunity management and pipeline progression.
  • Sales process automation.
  • Account and contact management.
  • Custom Lightning Web Components.
  • Integrations with external systems.
  • Reports and dashboards.
  • Data migration.
  • Territory and forecast management.

Role and contribution

Salesforce Technical Lead with hands-on development responsibilities. Contributed to implementation design and delivery across the confirmed functional scope, including custom UI, integrations, reporting and data migration.

Business value enabled

  • A more consolidated view of account, contact and opportunity information.
  • More consistent sales-process execution through automation and opportunity lifecycle management.
  • Better visibility for pipeline reviews, territory planning and forecasting.
  • Reporting and dashboards that bring sales information closer to operational decision-making.
  • A maintainable Salesforce foundation for future CRM enhancements and integrations.
