Case study 01 · Manufacturing

Enterprise Sales Cloud Implementation

An anonymized manufacturing CRM implementation focused on connected sales operations, pipeline visibility and forecasting.

Project overview

Implemented Salesforce Sales Cloud capabilities to support manufacturing sales operations, streamline the opportunity lifecycle, improve customer-record management and provide stronger visibility into pipeline and forecasting activities.

Confirmed implementation scope

  • Opportunity management and pipeline progression.
  • Sales process automation.
  • Account and contact management.
  • Custom Lightning Web Components.
  • Integrations with external systems.
  • Reports and dashboards.
  • Data migration.
  • Territory and forecast management.

Role and contribution

Salesforce Technical Lead with hands-on development responsibilities. Contributed to implementation design and delivery across the confirmed functional scope, including custom UI, integrations, reporting and data migration.

Business value

  • Centralized account, contact and opportunity information.
  • More consistent sales-process execution.
  • Improved pipeline, territory and forecast visibility.
  • Consolidated reporting and dashboard access for sales stakeholders.
  • A maintainable foundation for continued CRM enhancement.
